## Executive Summary

- Analyzed Airbnb marketplace data to identify the key drivers of occupancy, pricing, customer satisfaction, host performance, and revenue generation across listings, bookings, and customer reviews.
- Structured and analyzed a relational PostgreSQL database by integrating **Listings, Calendar,** and **Reviews** tables to perform end-to-end marketplace analytics.
- Applied advanced SQL concepts including **Joins, Aggregations, CTEs, Window Functions, Ranking Functions, CASE Statements,** and **Year-over-Year Analysis** to solve real-world business problems.
- Evaluated key business metrics including **Occupancy Rate, Average Nightly Price, Review Ratings, Host Performance, Revenue Distribution, Customer Engagement,** and **Seasonal Demand Trends** to measure marketplace performance.
- Identified that a **small percentage of listings generated a disproportionately high share of platform revenue**, highlighting high-value properties for premium marketing and pricing strategies.
- Found that **multi-property hosts** managed a significant share of marketplace inventory, emphasizing the importance of host retention and strategic partnership programs.
- Discovered that **highly rated listings consistently achieved stronger occupancy**, demonstrating the direct relationship between customer satisfaction and booking performance.
- Revealed that **premium-priced properties generated higher revenue despite lower booking frequency**, supporting differentiated pricing and revenue optimization strategies.
- Identified **customer review activity** as a leading indicator of listing popularity and engagement, while seasonal demand patterns highlighted opportunities for **dynamic pricing and inventory optimization**.
- Generated actionable business recommendations to improve **pricing strategy, occupancy optimization, host management, customer experience, and marketplace growth** through data-driven decision making.
