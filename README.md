## Executive Summary
  
Airbnb is a global vacation rental platform where booking trends, host quality, guest reviews, and pricing all shape overall marketplace performance. To understand what drives occupancy, revenue, and guest satisfaction, I analyzed Airbnb’s listings, calendar, and reviews data to identify patterns across property types, hosts, pricing, and customer engagement.
 
Study the dataset to evaluate property diversity, host engagement, guest ratings, booking trends, pricing patterns, review behavior, and year-over-year revenue growth, then turn those findings into practical business insights.

Used PostgreSQL to clean, structure, and analyze the data with joins, aggregations, subqueries, CTEs, ranking functions, and window functions. The analysis covered listing concentration, top-rated properties, host-level performance, occupancy rates, nightly pricing, review activity, revenue ranking, summer performance, and YoY growth to build a full view of marketplace behavior.

The analysis showed that a small group of listings drives a large share of revenue, highly rated properties tend to perform better on occupancy, and multi-property hosts contribute heavily to platform supply. It also highlighted clear pricing and seasonal demand patterns, along with review activity as a strong signal of guest engagement. These insights can support better pricing strategy, host management, listing optimization, and revenue planning.
